# Features

## 📊 Grade Charts

Visually track how your grade changes over time. See how each assignment affects your grade, how grade categories are broken down, and which new assignments have been posted since you last checked.

## 🧮 Grade Calculator (Hypothetical Mode)

Ask "what if?" without the anxiety:

See what your grade would be if you got a certain score on an upcoming assignment

Figure out exactly what you need on your final to reach your target grade

Add hypothetical assignments, change scores, or drop assignments entirely

Works on courses with and without grade categories

## 🔔 Attendance, Documents & Mail

Attendance broken down by day, showing exactly which periods you missed

View your report cards and school documents

Read your StudentVUE mail and download attachments

## 🔒 Private by Design

GradeCompass never sees your password or your grades. When you log in, your device connects directly to your school's StudentVUE portal — everything is computed client-side. 
Read the privacy page for details.

## 📦 Grades Data Export

Export your raw gradebook data (including the underlying XML) to inspect or back up your records.

## 🌙 Dark Mode & PWA

Full dark mode support, and installable as a Progressive Web App for a native app feel on your phone or desktop.
